Further Lalit Kala Akademi Awardees · Critical Notes

Forty further entries: the remaining names in the Akademi’s official “Last 5 Years NEA Awardees List” for NEA 61 (2019) and NEA 62 (2020), followed by ten named awardees from the official 65th NEA release. The source confirms names and exhibition years; where it does not publish a work title or medium, this page does not invent one.

Official 58–62 awardee list (Akademi PDF) ↗ · Official 65th NEA release ↗
