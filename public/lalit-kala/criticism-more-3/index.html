Historic Lalit Kala Akademi Awardees · Critical Notes

Twenty-five additional, officially documented award records: ten awardees of the 55th NEA (2014) and fifteen awardees of the 57th NEA (2016). These are older entries not included in the later roster pages. The annual reports provide the artist and awarded-work title; no unverified details are added.
