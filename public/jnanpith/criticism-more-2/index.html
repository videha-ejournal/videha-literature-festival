20 More Jnanpith Books · Set 3

21. The Shadow Lines · Amitav Ghosh

English

Memory, borders and family stories make national history intimate and unstable.

22. Tughlaq · Girish Karnad

Kannada

Malayalam

24. Sila Nerangalil Sila Manithargal · Jayakanthan

Tamil

Social judgement and female agency collide in a novel of stigma and self-definition.

25. Kosala · Bhalchandra Nemade

Marathi

A restless first-person voice makes education, place and alienation formally inseparable.

26. Pinjar · Amrita Pritam

Punjabi

Partition is experienced through a woman’s body, displacement and compromised belonging.

27. Pita Putra · Birendra Kumar Bhattacharya

Assamese

Family conflict becomes a lens on political change and moral inheritance.

28. Aapka Bunti · Mannu Bhandari

Hindi

A child’s fractured viewpoint exposes the emotional cost of adult separation.

29. Subarnalata · Ashapurna Devi

Bengali

Domestic detail becomes a long argument about women’s autonomy and social reform.

30. Saraswatichandra · Govardhanram Tripathi

Gujarati

The social novel maps reform, duty and the pressure of tradition.

31. Ekaveera · Viswanatha Satyanarayana

Telugu

Romance and honour are shaped by historical hierarchy and moral choice.

32. Paraja · Gopinath Mohanty

Odia

Land, debt and exploitation structure a powerful account of community dispossession.

33. Gul-e-Naghma · Firaq Gorakhpuri

Urdu

Lyric form holds classical inheritance and modern solitude in tension.

34. Siyah Rood · Rehman Rahi

Kashmiri

Poetry lets landscape carry both intimacy and political unease.

35. Śrī Bhārgavarāghavīyam · Jagadguru Rambhadracharya

Sanskrit

Epic form renews Sanskrit narrative through devotion, argument and disciplined craft.

36. Aamchi Maati Aamchi Manasa · Ravindra Kelekar

Konkani

Essays connect language, land and civic identity through a Konkani public voice.

37. Madhavi · Madan Mani Dixit

Nepali

Historical fiction tests desire, duty and the making of political identity.

38. Batan Ri Phulwari · Vijaydan Detha

Rajasthani

Oral storytelling turns village tale, wit and social critique into literary form.

39. Ain Jee · Narayan Shyam

Sindhi

Poetry makes exile, language and memory speak through compressed images.

40. Mithila Mahatmya · Vidyanath Jha Vidyapati

Maithili

The text’s sacred geography shows how place, devotion and literary memory are made together.
